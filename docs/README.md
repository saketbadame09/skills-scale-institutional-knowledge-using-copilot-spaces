# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

These documents provide repeatable guidance for delivering cross-functional work at OctoAcme—from validating an idea and planning delivery through release and learning. Use them as a shared reference for consistent execution, clear ownership, and effective onboarding.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme begins with initiation: validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define measurable outcomes, surface initial risks and resource needs, and decide whether the work is ready to proceed. Planning turns approved work into prioritized, estimated increments with clear acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, while mapping milestones, dependencies, and an initial QA approach.

During execution and tracking, teams manage work on a project board, deliver and review small increments, and monitor progress against milestones and success metrics. Daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos help surface blockers and keep the team aligned. Risk management and communication continue throughout the lifecycle: teams maintain and review a risk register, share status through a single source of truth, and escalate blockers through the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.

For release and deployment, the team confirms acceptance criteria are met, CI and security checks pass, and release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or mitigation plans are ready. The team deploys and verifies the change, communicates the release, and follows incident guidance if needed. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives capture what went well and what could improve, then track a small set of owned actions with due dates and success criteria.

The Project Manager (PM) coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; the Product Manager (PdM)/Product Lead defines outcomes and prioritizes work; developers design, build, test, and review solutions; QA/testing validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. These responsibilities work together to keep delivery customer-focused, transparent, and quality-oriented.
